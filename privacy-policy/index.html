Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Nimble Discover handles the information you share when you use this website and submit an application.

Information we collect

When you submit the application form, we collect:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address
  • Your mobile phone number
  • Your confirmation that we may contact you about the application

We also receive basic technical information that a web host normally logs, such as the browser type and the time of the visit. This site does not use advertising cookies or analytics cookies, and it does not ask for a Social Security number, bank details, or payment information.

How we use it

We use your application details to review your information, contact you about relevant opportunities, and keep a record of that conversation. We use them only for that recruitment purpose. An application does not guarantee placement, available work, or a particular income.

Who we share it with

Your details are shared with the recruitment team handling applications. A company that hosts this website, or a form service used to deliver the application, may process the information only so the team can receive it. We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep application details only as long as needed to review the application, follow up with you, and meet any legal record-keeping duty. When they are no longer needed, we delete or de-identify them.

Your choices

You can choose not to submit the form. If you have already applied and want to ask about, update, or request deletion of your information, use Contact Us or reply to the message you receive from the recruitment team.

Children

This website is for adults looking for flexible work. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
